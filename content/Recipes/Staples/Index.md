---
title: 1. Home-Made Mayonnaise
---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 3 eggs yolks
- [ ] 1 tsp dijon mustard
- [ ] 300 ml any neutral oil *(I used avocado)*
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Pepper
- [ ] Fresh lemon juice *(to taste)*

# Instructions
1. Place the ==egg yolks== and ==dijon mustard== into a food processor and blend for a couple seconds
2. While the food processor is running, VERY slowly begin to pour the first ==50 ml of oil== into the processor. This is important because this is when it is most likely to split. It should have the 'mayonnaise' consistency now
3. Slowly pour the rest of the ==oil== into the food processor.
4. After **all** the oil has been incorporated, add ==lemon juice==, ==salt==, and ==pepper== into mayonnaise
