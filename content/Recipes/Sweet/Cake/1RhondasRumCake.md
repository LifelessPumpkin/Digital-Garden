# Ingredients:
## Cake
- 1 Yellow cake mix
- 1 pkg Small vanilla instant pudding
- 4 Eggs
- 1/2 cup Water
- 1/2 cup Rum
- 1/2 cup Vegetable oil
## Glaze
- 1 cup Sugar
- 1/2 cup Melted butter
- 1/4 cup Rum
- 1/4 cup Water
# Instructions

1. ==The night before==: mix the sugar, butter, rum, and water and whisk to make the glaze and let sit overnight.
2. Preheat oven to 325° 
3. Mix in a large bowl, the yellow cake mix and instant pudding.
4. In another bowl mix the water, rum, vegetable oil, and eggs.
5. Mix the two bowls contents together
6. Bake for 45 minutes @ 325°
